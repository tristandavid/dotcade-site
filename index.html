Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems?

Send a message through the contact form and mention "Dotcade" in the subject. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

Restoring purchases: open Dotcade, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with.

Cancelling a subscription: on iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions.
